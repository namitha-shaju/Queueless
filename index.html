Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Queueless.

What is Queueless?

Queueless is a hospital queue management website designed to help patients plan their visits.

How do I find a department?

Go to the Departments section and choose the department you need.

Can I check my queue online?

Yes. Sign in to view your token and queue status.

Can I use Queueless on my phone?

Yes, the website layout adjusts to smaller screens.
